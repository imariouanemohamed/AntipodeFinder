ABOUT ANTIPODES

Geography made visual, useful, and memorable.

AntipodeFinder transforms a coordinate calculation into a geographic report. It shows the exact opposite point and adds useful context for locations that fall in the ocean, including an estimated nearest country, nearby populated place, and distance.
